Fire TV, Fire Stick, Android TV

You install it with the free Downloader app.

  1. Install Downloader (orange icon) from your TV's app store.
  2. Fire TV: open Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options → Install unknown apps and turn on Downloader.
    Android TV: when asked, allow Downloader to install apps from this source.
  3. Open Downloader and enter this code, then choose Go:

    3316691
  4. When the download finishes, choose Install, then Open.
Code not working? Use the web address instead

In Downloader's address box, type:
https://github.com/StreamLoomDownloads/streamloom-downloads/releases/download/stable/streamloom.apk

Android phone or tablet

  1. Tap the button below to download.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android says it is blocked, tap Settings and allow installs from your browser (or Files app), then go back.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.
Download Streamloom (APK) About 5 MB. Needs Android 7.0 or newer.

Google Play early access

Prefer Play Store updates? Join the early access group, then get the app from Play. You need the Google account you use on your phone.

  1. Join the group (free, approved automatically). Sign in with your Google account and tap Join group. A "no permission" message on the Conversations tab is normal; it only hides the discussion.
  2. Open the Play Store page below and tap Install. It can take a few minutes after joining before the button appears.
1. Join the early access group 2. Open on Google Play Play updates arrive automatically. A copy installed from Play can't be updated by the direct download above, and the other way round, so stick to one.

Updating

Install the newest file the same way. It updates over your existing copy and keeps your favourites and history. This link always points to the latest version.

Trouble?

"App not installed": make sure you have a little free space, and uninstall any older copy that came from somewhere else, then try again.
